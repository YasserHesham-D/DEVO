How We Started

From One Street
To Your Doorstep

DEVO Store started with a simple idea: bring world-class footwear to Sohag. Our founder, driven by a passion for quality and community, opened the doors of our flagship store on Sharia Al-Jumhuriya with just a handful of curated styles.

Today, we serve thousands of customers across Sohag Governorate — both from our beloved physical store and through our growing online platform. Every pair we sell carries the promise of authenticity, comfort, and style.

We don't just sell shoes. We help you walk confidently through every chapter of your life.
